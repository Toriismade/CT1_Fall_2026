Stardust Heritage

The heavy elements in our bodies (carbon, nitrogen, oxygen, iron) were not created at the dawn of the universe, but forged inside the cores of ancient, massive stars and scattered across space via supernovas."We are made of star-stuff." The atoms in your body share the same origin as those in a galaxy billions of light-years away.
