Before We Blink

A Time Storm has trapped the Time Lords and the Weeping Angels in the same small pocket of time. Both sides want one thing: time energy.

The Time Lords need Zeiton Crystals to charge the TARDIS and escape. The Angels want to feed on the time energy inside every Time Lord.

The storm reaches the TARDIS in 3:00. If the Time Lords have not escaped by then, the Angels win.

Time Lords

  • Carry every crystal to the console.
  • Angels in their view cannot move.
  • Carrying a crystal switches the view off. It cannot be put down.
  • Each starts with 3 energy and can give it to a Time Lord it touches. At 0 it is out.

Weeping Angels

  • Drain every Time Lord, or outlast the storm.
  • They only move while no one is looking at them.
  • Each touch takes 1 energy.
  • After feeding, that Angel freezes for 10 seconds.

Both teams run on their own. Set the conditions in the panel and watch how they change the result.
